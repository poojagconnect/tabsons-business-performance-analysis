
# Tabsons – Business Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
A Founder’s Office business case project demonstrating financial analysis, business performance monitoring and strategic recommendations for a simulated manufacturing business.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial and business analysis
- Data visualization
- PowerPoint

## Project Scope
- 12-month revenue and cost analysis
- Product-wise profitability
- Profit margin analysis
- Material cost monitoring
- Management recommendations

## Key Findings
- Revenue increased throughout the illustrative period.
- Profit growth did not keep pace with revenue growth.
- Sheet Metal Parts had the lowest product margin.
- Engineering Assemblies contributed the largest share of profit.

## Recommendations
- Protect profit margins before increasing sales volume.
- Review pricing and material costs.
- Prioritize higher-margin product categories.
- Monitor monthly business performance through MIS reporting.
